# WP6: Validation and Operations

* **Monte Carlo - Quoted Errors:** The spread of the pull (`(recovered - injected) / quoted error`) is below 1.0. This proves the software's quoted uncertainties are conservative and reliable; the system is not overconfident in its fits.
* **Monte Carlo - Velocity Bias:** The mean pull is approximately +0.8, which translates to a systematic bias of about +15 km/s. This offset is an artifact of the width prior ($245$ km/s) disagreeing with the actual injected physical width ($229$ km/s), artificially dragging the center measurement. 
* **Monte Carlo - Marginal Detection:** Around a third of the seeds fail the $S/N \ge 5$ check for Hydrogen. The system is operating correctly, but the physical signal is simply marginal at this 1.5m dish size given the thermal noise.
* **Stress Test Breaking Point:** When subjecting the observatory to harsh wind, a 5.0 km catalogue error, and 3x pointing errors, the system collapses. The satellite efficiency drops to ~0.08 and the true sky error remains stuck at $\sim0.2^\circ$. Reverting the settings one by one reveals that the **3.0m Dish Size** is the primary breaking point. The larger dish narrows the beam from $1.25^\circ$ to $0.62^\circ$, meaning the initial $3\times$ pointing errors fall entirely outside the beam's capture range during the calibration scans, preventing the model from fitting successfully.
